Privacy & Informed Consent Notice

1. Ephemeral Image Processing: Camera frames are captured only when you trigger a scan or proximity event. Full-resolution frames are transmitted over encrypted TLS directly to Google Gemini and are wiped from browser memory immediately after inference.

2. Local Data Storage: Your Gemini API key and scan history are stored strictly within your browser's private localStorage. No personal data is sent to Third Eye creators.

3. Emergency Dispatches: SOS emergency alerts are routed to your local dashboard and configured caregiver endpoints. Obstacle proximity warnings remain local to prevent false caregiver alarms.

4. Mobility Disclaimer: Third Eye is an experimental assistive aid. It does NOT replace a white cane or guide dog. Never navigate independently in dangerous areas solely relying on electronic sensors.
